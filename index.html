    Your domain × AI project

    A way to pick one AI project from work you already know. Use it to choose and scope the featured project on your homepage.

    Skip the tutorial projects everyone builds. Build a small tool for a task from your own work, using public or synthetic data. Knowing a niche problem well is an advantage: few people can build for it.

    Your domainA task you know well that is slow, repetitive or error-prone
    ×
    An AI patternOne of the six below
    =
    Your projectSmall, working and measured. It becomes your featured project.
    • Extract Pull structured fields out of messy documents
    • Ask Answer questions from documents, with sources
    • Triage Sort and prioritize items, with a reason
    • Draft Write a first version for a person to review
    • Check Review a document or config against rules
    • Act An agent that runs several steps with tools

    Pick your background

    Starting points only. Swap in a task you've done yourself; that's what makes the project yours.

    Build it in a weekend

    1. Pick one task from your own work: one input, one output.
    2. Use safe data: public or synthetic. Never your employer's, a customer's or a patient's.
    3. Build the smallest version with your coding assistant, in your class project.
    4. Measure it: 20 to 30 test cases you check by hand. Note accuracy, time and cost per run.
    5. Write it up as a case study (see Upgrade 2).
    6. Feature it at the top of your homepage and post about it on LinkedIn.

    What makes it convincing

    • Numbers: how many test cases, accuracy, cost per run, time saved
    • One case it gets wrong, and what you changed
    • A live demo or a 60-second video
    • How you built it: the coding assistant, the model, what you checked by hand
    • Answers that cite their sources
    • A plain statement that the data is public or synthetic

    Prompt for your coding assistant

    I want one AI portfolio project that combines my domain with AI.
My background: [ROLE, INDUSTRY, YEARS]. Tasks I know well: [2-3 TASKS]. Target role: [ROLE].
1. Suggest five project ideas. For each: the task, the AI pattern (extract, ask, triage, draft, check or act), a public or synthetic dataset I can legally use, and how I'd measure that it works.
2. Rank them by what a hiring manager for [ROLE] would find most convincing.
3. For the top one, list the smallest version I can build in a weekend inside this Next.js project.
Don't suggest using my employer's or any customer's data.

    Upgrade 1: Lead with your story

    1–2 hoursFree
    Do this

    Rewrite the top of your homepage. Show the role you want, one line of proof, where you're based (and remote, hybrid or on-site), and two buttons: Download resume and Book 15 minutes.

    Why

    Someone skimming your site wants to know what role you want, what you've done and how to reach you. Answer that before the first scroll.

    Pick the one that fits you

    Moving into AILead with your domain × AI project.
    Between rolesTarget role in the headline, "Available now", resume and booking link.
    Senior ICThree or four case studies with scale and numbers, recent work first.
    LeaderTeams led, decisions made and what changed. Writing or talks.
    Back from a breakRecent courses and projects with dates, and one honest line about the break.
    Steps
    1. Headline: your name and target role, in words a recruiter would search ("Senior Data Engineer", not "Data Wrangler").
    2. Proof: one line with a number ("Cut pipeline costs 40% at a 2,000-person fintech").
    3. "Work with me" section: roles you're targeting; years, domains, stack and certifications; a booking link (Cal.com has a free plan for individuals; Google Calendar appointment schedules also work); two or three LinkedIn recommendations, with permission.
    4. Resume PDF: save it in public/ as firstname-lastname-resume.pdf. Remove your home address, and your phone number unless you want calls. Keep it consistent with the site.

    Upgrade 2: Turn projects into case studies

    1–2 hours eachFree
    Do this

    Give your two best projects their own page, starting with your domain × AI project.

    Why

    A project card says what you built. A case study shows how you think, which is what a hiring manager is judging.

    Every case study has six parts

    1 · ProblemOne or two sentences anyone can follow.
    2 · Your roleWhat you did. Say "I".
    3 · ApproachTwo or three key decisions and why.
    4 · ResultA number: time, cost, accuracy, users.
    5 · What's nextWhat you'd change or add.
    6 · EvidenceScreenshots, a short video, GitHub, a live link.
    Steps
    1. Start with the class project. The Job Fit Analyzer, Cover Letter Generator and Interview Prep tools make a real case study: a Next.js app with login, server-side model calls and a provider-neutral AI helper. Add a short screen recording; keep the tools themselves private.
    2. Work you can't share (it belongs to an employer): describe the problem and your decisions in general terms, leave out the company's numbers, names and code, and say "Details changed to protect client data."
    Prompt for your coding assistant 
    Add case-study pages for my projects. Use the projects in data/profile.json.
1. Create /projects/[slug] pages. If I'm on GitHub Pages, use
   generateStaticParams so it still exports as static files.
2. Each page has: Problem, My role, Approach, Result, What I'd do next, Evidence.
   Add those fields to data/profile.json; leave them empty where I haven't
   given you facts, and don't invent results or numbers.
3. Link each homepage project card to its page.
Then ask me five questions to fill in the first case study.

    Upgrade 3: Use your own domain name

    30 minutesAbout $10–12 a year
    Do this

    Buy firstnamelastname.com (or .dev or .me if it's taken) and point it at your site. Avoid hyphens and numbers.

    Why

    yourname.com on a resume looks deliberate, and the link stays the same if you later move from GitHub Pages to Vercel.

    Pick one registrar (a .com, per year)

    Cloudflare RegistrarAbout $10.50. Sold at cost; free email forwarding.Lowest price
    PorkbunAbout $11, the same price every year.Simplest dashboard
    Vercel DomainsPrice shown at checkout. Connects itself.Easiest on Vercel
    NamecheapAbout $11 the first year, then about $18.70.Watch the renewal
    Steps

    On Vercel (Vercel guide):

    1. Project → Settings → Domains → Add Domain. Add yourname.com and www.yourname.com.
    2. At your registrar, add the A and CNAME records Vercel shows for your project.

    On GitHub Pages (GitHub guide):

    1. Repository → Settings → Pages → Custom domain. Enter it and Save before you add DNS records.
    2. At your registrar, add four A records for the domain: 185.199.108.153, 185.199.109.153, 185.199.110.153, 185.199.111.153.
    3. Add a CNAME record for www pointing to USERNAME.github.io.
    4. When the site loads, tick Enforce HTTPS. Because you publish with GitHub Actions, no CNAME file is needed.
    The one thing that breaks

    The class Pages version set basePath: "/portfolio". On your own domain, set it to "", or styles and links break.

    DNS changes can take up to 24 hours, usually minutes. Optional: forward hello@yourname.com to your Gmail for free with Cloudflare Email Routing or Porkbun. It receives mail only.

    Prompt for your coding assistant 
    I bought [yourname.com] and I host my public portfolio on [GitHub Pages / Vercel].
1. Check next.config.ts. If I'm on GitHub Pages with a custom domain,
   basePath must be "". Fix it and any links that assumed /[repo-name].
2. List the exact DNS records I need to add at [registrar], and where
   in their dashboard. Don't guess values that my host shows me; tell me
   where to read them.
3. After I confirm the records are added, help me verify with dig
   (Mac) or Resolve-DnsName (Windows) and check https:// works.

    Upgrade 4: Make shared links look right

    30 minutesFree
    Do this

    Add a page title, a description, a share image and a small site icon. Test the preview on LinkedIn, then tell Google about your site.

    Why

    When you share your link on LinkedIn, Slack or email, people see a preview card first. Without these, it shows as a bare URL.

    Steps
    1. Title and description in src/app/layout.tsx: Your Name | Target Role and your one-line pitch.
    2. Share image: opengraph-image.png, 1200×630, in src/app/. A static file works on GitHub Pages and Vercel.
    3. Site icon: src/app/icon.png or icon.svg.
    4. Sitemap: sitemap.ts and robots.ts in src/app/.
    5. Structured data: JSON-LD marking your homepage as a ProfilePage about a Person, linked to your LinkedIn and GitHub.
    6. Test in LinkedIn Post Inspector. Run it again after each change; posts already published keep their old preview.
    7. Tell Google in Search Console: add your domain, verify it with the DNS record it gives you, and submit /sitemap.xml.

    Reference: Next.js Metadata and OG images.

    Prompt for your coding assistant 
    Add SEO and sharing metadata to my portfolio. My site is [https://yourname.com],
hosted on [GitHub Pages / Vercel].
1. In src/app/layout.tsx set title "[Name] | [Role]", a description from my pitch
   in data/profile.json, and metadataBase.
2. Create a 1200x630 opengraph-image.png in src/app with my name and role
   (a static file, so it works with static export). Use my photo if I have one in public/.
3. Add an icon, sitemap.ts and robots.ts. Keep them static if I use GitHub Pages.
4. Add JSON-LD for a ProfilePage whose mainEntity is a Person with name, jobTitle,
   image, and sameAs links to my LinkedIn and GitHub from data/profile.json.
Then build, and show me the <head> tags from the built homepage.

    Upgrade 5: Link your site from LinkedIn

    30 minutesFree
    Do this

    Add your site to LinkedIn's Contact info and Featured section, end your About with it, and post about what you built.

    Why

    Recruiters look you up on LinkedIn. Your site should be one click from your profile, with the preview from Upgrade 4.

    Steps
    1. Contact info → Add website. You can add up to three. Choose type Portfolio, or Other to write your own label. LinkedIn help
    2. Featured → Add → Add a link. Add your site, then your best case study. LinkedIn help
    3. About: end with "Projects and case studies: yourname.com".
    4. Projects on each relevant job: link the case study.
    5. Post a short note on what you built and what you learned.
    6. Custom button ("Visit my website"): only for some Premium Business, Sales Navigator and Recruiter Lite subscribers. LinkedIn help

    Also add it to: your resume header, your GitHub profile and profile README, your email signature, and the Website field on job applications.

    Prompt for your coding assistant 
    Write three versions of a short LinkedIn post announcing my portfolio at
[yourname.com]. I'm targeting [ROLE]. Mention one thing I built with AI and
one thing I learned building the site. Under 120 words, no hashtag spam,
no emoji walls. Then write the one-line "About" ending that links to the site.

    Upgrade 6: See who visits

    15 minutesFree
    Do this

    Add one analytics tool. To see which buttons people click (resume, email, LinkedIn, booking), pick one that tracks clicks.

    Why

    After you apply, a visit from LinkedIn or a company's network tells you someone looked, and which pages they read.

    Pick one

    Vercel Web AnalyticsFree on Hobby: 50,000 events a month, one month of history. Clicks need a paid plan.Best on Vercel (visits only)
    Cloudflare Web AnalyticsFree, works on any host with one script tag, no cookies.Best on GitHub Pages
    Umami CloudFree Hobby plan, about 100,000 events a month, any host.Best for click tracking
    Google Analytics 4Free, any host. Uses cookies.If you already use it
    Steps
    1. Create an account with the tool and add your site.
    2. Paste its script into src/app/layout.tsx (the prompt below does this).
    3. Use a different link in each place, such as yourname.com/?ref=linkedin and ?ref=resume, to see where visitors came from.
    4. Don't collect visitors' personal data.

    Sources: Vercel, Cloudflare, Umami.

    Prompt for your coding assistant 
    Add [Cloudflare Web Analytics / Umami / Vercel Web Analytics] to my portfolio.
I host on [GitHub Pages / Vercel]. Put the script in src/app/layout.tsx so every
page is counted. Then add click tracking on: resume download, email, LinkedIn,
and booking link, if my tool supports custom events. Don't add cookies or
collect personal data. Tell me what I need to paste from the tool's dashboard.

    Upgrade 7: Make it look like you

    1–3 hoursFree
    Do this

    Choose one accent color and a pair of fonts, add dark mode and a clear headshot, and check the site on your phone.

    Why

    The class template is plain on purpose. A few design choices make it yours without pulling attention from the content.

    Pick one

    Restyle what you haveKeep everything you built; change colors, fonts and spacing.Recommended
    Portfolio Starter KitVercel's minimal template with a blog, sitemap and share images built in.
    Magic UI portfolioMinimal, with subtle motion. MIT license; all content in one file.
    More templatesVercel's portfolio gallery. Check each license.
    Steps
    1. Pick one accent color plus neutrals that pass contrast checks.
    2. Load a heading and a body font with next/font. Inter, Geist and IBM Plex are safe choices.

   that follows the system setting, with a toggle.
3. Check 375px and 1440px widths and tell me anything that looks off.

    A weekendPay only for model use
    Do this

    Add one AI feature visitors can use. Then, if they fit your story, add one or two extra pages.

    Why

    Pick one AI feature

    Rules for AI features
    1. Answer only from facts you approved in data/profile.json; otherwise say "I don't know".

    Prompt for your coding assistant

4. No storing of visitor messages.
Write tests for the "not in my profile" case and the rate limit, then run them.

    Checklist and upkeep

    One check for your project and one per upgrade, plus a monthly routine. Use it to know when you're done and to keep the site current.

    Ticks are saved in this browser only.

    Once a month, 15 minutes

    • Update your headline, the "Now" section and anything with a date. Out-of-date dates undercut everything else.
    • Add one new piece of evidence: a project, a result, a post or a certificate.
    • Click every link and button, and download your own resume.
    • Check analytics: where visitors came from and what they read.
    • Open the site on your phone.
